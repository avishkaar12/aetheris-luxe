House Backstory

From Paris Vaults To Modern Icons

Founded in 1894 as a private scent atelier for collectors, Aetheris evolved from handwritten formulas and hand-cut crystal to a new era of haute perfumery. Every release honors one promise: precision, rarity, and emotional depth.

1894

First private formula composed in Rue de Rivoli.

1958

Archive cellar opened for numbered collector editions.

2026

Aetheris launches the digital luxury boutique experience.
